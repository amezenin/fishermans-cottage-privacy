Privacy Policy for Fisherman's Cottage

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fisherman's Cottage ("the Game"), developed and published by Mezhenon Production, handles information when you use the Game.

1. Information We Collect

Fisherman's Cottage does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.

Game progress, preferences, language settings, sound settings, progression data, and similar gameplay information may be stored locally on your device.

2. Advertising

Fisherman's Cottage may display advertisements using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Advertising providers may automatically collect or process certain information in order to provide, measure, personalize, and protect advertising services. This may include:

  • IP address
  • Device and advertising identifiers
  • Device information
  • Advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Approximate location derived from information such as IP address

The exact information processed may depend on your device, region, privacy choices, and the advertising services available to you.

You can learn more about how Google handles information through its advertising services here:

Google Privacy Policy

How Google uses information for advertising

3. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, including in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland, consent or privacy choices may be requested before certain advertising technologies are used.

Depending on your region and privacy choices, you may receive personalized, non-personalized, or limited advertisements.

Privacy and advertising choices may also be managed through interfaces provided by Google or the operating system where applicable.

4. Rewarded Advertisements

The Game may offer optional rewarded advertisements. Watching these advertisements may provide in-game benefits, such as additional rewards, bonuses, or other gameplay advantages.

Rewarded advertisements are optional and are not required to access the basic gameplay of Fisherman's Cottage.

5. In-App Purchases

Fisherman's Cottage may offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing, including a one-time Premium upgrade.

All payment processing is handled by Google Play. Mezhenon Production does not directly collect or store payment card numbers, banking information, or other payment credentials.

Google Play may process information related to purchases and transactions in accordance with Google's own privacy policies and terms.

The Game may receive or verify purchase status and entitlement information from Google Play in order to determine whether purchased features, such as Premium, should be enabled.

6. Age Restrictions and Children's Privacy

Fisherman's Cottage is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

Mezhenon Production does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the Game.

7. Local Game Data

The Game may store gameplay data locally on your device, including save data, settings, progression, and preferences.

Removing the Game or clearing its application data may permanently delete locally stored progress.

8. Third-Party Services

Fisherman's Cottage may use third-party services necessary for advertising, in-app purchases, game distribution, diagnostics, or platform functionality.

These third-party services operate according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Third-party services used by the Game may include:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Play services and distribution infrastructure

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to minimize the collection of personal information and rely on established platform and service providers where third-party processing is required.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Game's functionality, advertising systems, in-app purchase systems, third-party services, or legal requirements change.

Updates will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.
